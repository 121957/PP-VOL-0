Borzois, Birdies
강아지들이 노는 숲은 좋은 숲이다. 원래는 그레이하운드가 아닐까 하고 생각하며 다가갔었는데, 말처럼 커다란, 그리고 개미핥기처럼 주둥이가 긴 보르조이로 결론지었다. 처음엔 수를 알 수 없어서 세 마리인가 보다 하였고, 한 마리가 되었다. 극본을 번역할 때 '로빈 새들'로 부를지 고민하다, 울새들로 적었다. 극본만큼은 가능한 각 언어에 충실하게 옮기려고 한다. 울새들은 굉장히 동그랗다.
-
The forest where dogs are playing is a good forest. When I approached the dogs I was thinking if they were grey-hounds, and at the end I confirmed that they were Borzois that are as large as horses, having long snouts like anteaters. Since I couldn't count them at first, I guessed there were three dogs, and now there is only one. As for the Robin birds, when I was translating scripts I was thinking about a Korean translation of Robin birds - whether they should be described as 'Robin' birds literally or translated into their Korean name -, and decided to translate the name because I wanted both scripts to stick to their respective languages. The Robins, by the way, are very round.
